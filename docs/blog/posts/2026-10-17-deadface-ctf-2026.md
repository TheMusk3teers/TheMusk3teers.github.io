# Riding into DEADFACE CTF 2026

Our first campaign as a team: DEADFACE CTF 2026, from 17 to 19 October.

<!-- more -->

DEADFACE is an online, story-driven jeopardy CTF run by Cyber Hacktics. Challenges follow a fictional hacker group, so clues from one challenge often help with another.

We play as **4Musketeers**. Writeups go up on the [campaign page](../../ctfs/deadface-2026/index.md) once the competition closes.
